# Job Fit Scoring Rubric

Use this document to score a job posting 1-5 on fit. Return a score, 2-3 sentences of reasoning, and any flags.

## Target titles (strong signal)
- VP Commercial, SVP Commercial, Chief Commercial Officer
- GM North America
- VP Sales & Marketing
- Head of Commercial Operations
- Head of GTM / VP GTM / Director/VP Commercial Excellence
- Head of Product
- VP Commercial / CCO, AI-Enabled Care Delivery
- Head of Commercial, AI Health Platform / Digital Health
- VP GTM, Healthcare AI
- Chief Commercial Officer, Clinical AI / AI-Driven Diagnostics
- VP Clinical Partnerships / VP Health System Partnerships (AI health-tech context)
- Operating Partner, Commercial Advisor, Portfolio Executive (PE/VC context only)

## Seniority and scope
- Prefers people-manager, org-building roles. Track record is standing up teams from scratch.
- Individual contributor (IC) roles are acceptable but not preferred. Flag explicitly as "IC role" so it can be weighed separately, don't auto-reject.
- 15+ years experience expected as a baseline in the posting's requirements.

## Must-haves (any one of these is a strong positive)
- MedTech or medical device company, $10M-$150M revenue
- Regulated, clinical-sales environment (surgeons, hospital systems, KOLs), not transactional B2B
- European or international parent company standing up US/Canada commercial function
- Post-merger or post-acquisition integration underway
- Company building AI-driven clinical or commercial tools sold into hospitals/surgeons

**Clarification:** digital health companies with licensed clinical staff actually delivering care (physical therapists, physicians, etc.), not just software, count toward the regulated clinical-sales must-have, even if the buyer is an employer or health plan rather than a hospital or surgeon directly.

## Nice-to-haves (push score up, not required)
- Private equity or strategic-owned, not yet public
- Orthopedics, surgical devices, implants, craniomaxillofacial, organ transplant/perfusion
- Explicit mention of "building commercial infrastructure from the ground up"
- Healthcare-exclusive PE fund with transatlantic (US/Europe) platform

## Keywords to check for in posting text
GTM strategy, commercial operations, commercial infrastructure, post-merger integration, CRM adoption, forecast accuracy, sales enablement, compensation design, compliance/Sunshine Act, hospital adoption, clinical GTM, competitive intelligence, market opportunity mapping

## Deal-breakers (score 1-2, flag clearly)
- Pure digital health / SaaS role with no clinical or regulated-device component
- Public company role requiring investor relations or public-market experience
- Requires direct investing or deal-execution experience (associate/principal-level PE)
- Non-healthcare industry
- Requires a technical AI/data-science background (not commercial leadership of an AI product)

## Scoring guide
- **5**: Matches a target title, at least one must-have present, no deal-breakers.
- **4**: Matches target title or close variant, but either no must-have is clearly present, or there's a minor gap (e.g. IC role, slightly outside core sector but adjacent).
- **3**: Plausible fit needing real framing (see profile doc: PE operating partner, commercial excellence/advisory, broader MedTech outside transplant/perfusion).
- **2**: Weak fit, one or more deal-breakers present but some redeeming signal (e.g. healthcare-adjacent).
- **1**: Deal-breaker present, but at least one must-have or nice-to-have offsets it partially.
- **0 (Non-fit)**: Deal-breaker present, with zero must-haves and zero nice-to-haves offsetting it. Route to the "Non-fits" list, not the main scored list. Still log the company/title/date and a one-line reason, in case criteria shift later, but don't surface it for regular review.

## Handling ambiguity
If a posting could reasonably land on either side of a must-have or deal-breaker line, don't force a single confident score. Give a best-guess score, then add a one-line flag in this format:

`Ambiguous: could also score [X] if [specific interpretation] applies.`

Example: a digital health company selling to employers, with licensed clinical staff, might read as "Ambiguous: could also score 5 if digital health with licensed clinical delivery counts as regulated clinical sales; could also score 2 if treated as pure digital health SaaS."

Postings with an ambiguity flag should be routed to a separate "needs review" view in Notion, not the main scored list, so the primary list stays limited to postings the rubric could confidently resolve.

## Routing summary
- **Scores 1-5**: main scored list, sorted high to low.
- **Ambiguity flag present**: "Needs review" view, regardless of score.
- **Score 0**: "Non-fits" view, logged but not surfaced for regular review.

## Notes
This rubric is the fast-filter layer. For anything scoring 3+, refer back to the full Profile details document for pitch angle, framing, and honest-gap language before drafting outreach or tailoring materials.
